\vspace{0.10cm}

\begin{block}{\scriptsize Overall Trends}
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Turbulence:} $k$--$\omega$ formulations are most common (\textbf{55.6\%}), followed by SA-based models (\textbf{44.4\%}).
    \item \textbf{Droplets:} Predominantly Eulerian (\textbf{66.7\%}), with \textbf{33.3\%} Lagrangian.
    \item \textbf{Heat transfer:} Documented methods include $T_{rec}$ (\textbf{50\%}), BL integral (\textbf{25\%}), and two-$T_w$ (\textbf{25\%}).
    \item \textbf{Thermodynamics:} Messinger-based models predominate (\textbf{75\%} of documented submissions).
    \item \textbf{Geometry evolution:} Diverse approaches are used, with Lagrangian displacement methods most common.
    \item \textbf{Ice accretion:} Workshop comparisons focus on single-shot results; \textbf{4 participants} also submitted multilayer/multishot results, examined separately later.
\end{itemize}
\end{block}
