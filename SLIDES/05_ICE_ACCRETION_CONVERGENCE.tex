\begin{frame}{\small NACA0012 -- Ice Accretion Assessment}
\scriptsize

\begin{block}{\scriptsize Ice-Mass Consistency}
\begin{itemize}
    \item Ice mass shows limited grid sensitivity and good L1 agreement ($\mathrm{IQR}\approx5\%$); horn orientation is more variable, while 15- and 7-bin results are comparable.
\end{itemize}
\end{block}

\vspace{0.10cm}

\begin{block}{\scriptsize Response to Icing Condition}
\begin{itemize}
    \item AE3933 predicts less ice than AE3932, but underestimates the experimental $\sim15\%$ reduction.
    \item The smaller AE3933 upper-horn angle is consistent with experiment.
\end{itemize}
\end{block}

\vspace{0.10cm}

\begin{block}{\scriptsize Ice-Shape Prediction}
\begin{itemize}
    \item Horn angle exhibits substantial code-to-code variability and sensitivity to spatial refinement, but limited sensitivity to droplet-bin discretization.
    \item Limited multilayer results suggest improved ice-shape agreement, but remain insufficient for firm conclusions.
\end{itemize}
\end{block}

\vspace{0.10cm}

\begin{alertblock}{\scriptsize Ice Accretion Takeaway}
Predictions capture the experimental AE3932--AE3933 trends but underestimate the ice-mass difference, with substantial variability remaining in the final ice geometry.
\end{alertblock}

\end{frame}
