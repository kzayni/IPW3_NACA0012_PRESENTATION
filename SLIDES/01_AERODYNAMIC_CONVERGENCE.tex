\vspace{0.15cm}

\begin{block}{\scriptsize Observations}
\begin{itemize}
    \item At L1, the median drag coefficient is $C_D=0.01088$ (\textbf{108.8 drag counts}), with an IQR of \textbf{7.57 drag counts} ($7.0\%$).
    \item $C_D$ exhibits the greatest sensitivity to spatial grid refinement among the integrated aerodynamic coefficients.
    \item On the coarser grids, most submissions deviate by up to approximately \textbf{30\%} from their respective L1 values.
    \item One submission additionally exhibits pronounced non-monotonic behavior with grid refinement.
\end{itemize}
\end{block}

\end{frame}

\end{itemize}

\begin{frame}{\small NACA0012 -- Aerodynamic Assessment Summary}
\scriptsize

\begin{block}{\scriptsize L1 Code-to-Code Variability}
\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{lccc}
\toprule
 & \textbf{Median} & \textbf{IQR} & \textbf{Relative IQR} \\
\midrule
$C_L$ & $0.4677$    & $0.0262$                 & $5.6\%$ \\
$C_D$ & $108.8$ counts & $7.57$ counts          & $7.0\%$ \\
$C_M$ & $\approx 0$ & $2.60\times10^{-3}$      & -- \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

\vspace{0.05cm}

\centering
\textit{Relative IQR is not reported for $C_M$ because its median is close to zero.}
\end{block}

\vspace{0.15cm}

\begin{block}{\scriptsize Grid-Refinement Behavior}
\begin{itemize}
    \item $C_L$ and $C_M$ exhibit limited within-code sensitivity to grid refinement for most submissions.
    \item $C_D$ shows greater and less uniform grid sensitivity, including non-monotonic behavior for selected submissions.
    \item For most submissions, code-to-code differences exceed the changes produced by spatial refinement.
\end{itemize}
\end{block}

\vspace{0.15cm}

\begin{alertblock}{\scriptsize Aerodynamic Takeaway}
Overall, the aerodynamic predictions are well clustered, with L1 $C_p$ distributions showing good agreement with experiment despite differences in prescribed surface roughness. Among the integrated coefficients, $C_D$ exhibits the greatest sensitivity to spatial grid refinement.
\end{alertblock}

\end{frame}
