\begin{block}{\scriptsize Overall Assessment}
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Aerodynamics:} Predictions are well clustered; $C_p$ agrees well with experiment, while $C_D$ shows the greatest grid sensitivity.

    \item \textbf{Droplet Impingement:} Limited grid sensitivity is observed, with 7-bin results closely reproducing the 15-bin reference.

    \item \textbf{HTC \& Thermodynamics:} $HTC$ exhibits substantial code-to-code variability, with roughness playing only a partial role; the warmer AE3933 condition produces a broader low-freezing-fraction region near stagnation.

    \item \textbf{Ice Accretion:} AE3933 predicts less ice and a smaller upper-horn angle, consistent with experiment, but substantially underpredicts the observed ice-mass reduction; considerable shape variability remains.
\end{itemize}
\end{block}

\vspace{0.15cm}

\begin{alertblock}{\scriptsize Overall Takeaway}
Aerodynamics and impingement are comparatively consistent across submissions, while variability increases through thermodynamics and final ice geometry. The AE3932--AE3933 experimental trends are captured qualitatively, but their magnitude remains underpredicted.
\end{alertblock}
